Soft feedback primitives in the designMe brand.

```jsx
<Badge tone="sage">New</Badge>
<Tag>soft romantic</Tag>
<Toast tone="sage" icon={<CheckIcon />}>Saved</Toast>
```

- **Badge** — uppercase status/count pill. Tones: `neutral` / `sage` / `terra` / `ink`.
- **Tag** — tiny editorial eyebrow chip; demotes a trend/jargon word under a concrete label.
- **Toast** — gentle confirmation pill. Tones: `ink` / `sage`. Drive visibility with `show`.
